✨ Creator's Guide — How to Play God

🪐

1. Explore the Multi-Scale Cosmos

Switch between Universe, Galaxy, System and Surface in the top-left HUD (keys 1-4, Backspace goes up). In space, click a galaxy, star or planet to fly to it.

🌍

2. Descend to the Living Surface

Click "Descend to Surface" on any planet to explore its biomes, wildlife and clans. The surface starts uncluttered: press H (or the panels button) for the world overview, planet map and resources. Esc closes any open window.

👑

3. The God Creation Workshop

Click Workshop in the power bar (or Create Life Here on a planet). Raise a champion guided by ✦ Laya AI, design a species from genes and watch its founders breed and evolve, or forge a brand new planet.

⛰️

4. Shape Worlds & Cast Divine Powers

Select divine powers from the bottom toolbar to raise mountains, carve ocean trenches, bless crops with rain, or hurl cosmic meteors.

⚡

5. Control Time & Watch Civilizations Evolve

Warp time to 100x or 10,000x in the top-right HUD to watch continents drift, ice ages advance, species mutate, and civilizations rise, collapse into ruins, and be reborn!

🛐

6. Faiths and the Stars

Mortals never learn you exist. They invent gods from their land and from what you do to them, build shrines, convert each other and split into sects. Cast Starward Vision (Cosmic powers) on a people and its spaceport will send colony ships with real settlers to other planets and stars.
