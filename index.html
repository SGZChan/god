1. Explore the Multi-Scale Cosmos

Switch smoothly between 🌌 Galaxy, 🪐 Solar System, and 🌍 Planetary Surface in the top-left HUD. In 3D space, click on any planet to orbit and focus on it.

Click "Descend to Surface" on any planet to explore its procedural biomes, wildlife, tribes, and continental drift in real time.
